Reliability & polish — issues found in review

Items found during the source review. Fixed in v1.0.3: settings now persist, Wacom eraser-flip works, the About dialog shows the version with a Check-for-Updates button, and the right-click / context-menu / status-bar-clipping bugs are resolved. Remaining items:

  • High Autosave can silently fail on real projects. Autosave writes the whole project to localStorage as base64 PNGs (src/io.js); raster scenes quickly exceed the ~5 MB quota and autosave() just returns false with no warning. The desktop build should autosave to a file on disk.
  • Medium WebM export is real-time. Export.webm drives a MediaRecorder with await wait(dur) per frame — slow for long scenes and prone to dropped / duplicated frames; it also carries no audio.
  • Low Light pen pressure snaps to 0.5. e.pressure || 0.5 treats a genuine near-zero reading as half pressure.
  • Low Dead condition. if (e.ctrlKey || true) in the wheel handler (src/canvas.js) is vestigial.
  • Low No global error surface. Errors land in the console only; an unsigned installer also triggers a one-time SmartScreen warning.
